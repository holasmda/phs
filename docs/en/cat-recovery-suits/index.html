Recovery suits for spayed & neutered cats

The alternative to the cone of shame: a soft cotton suit that protects the incision and lets your cat eat, sleep and move normally.

Goodbye, cone of shame

The plastic cone stresses cats out: they can't eat properly, they bump into everything and they can't sleep comfortably. The recovery suit covers the surgical incision without immobilizing your cat — they can jump, curl up and use the litter box normally.

Breathable, non-toxic cotton

Soft cotton fabric, no chemicals or harsh dyes, in direct contact with freshly operated skin. Breathable so the incision heals well, and washable to keep it clean through the recovery days.

Made to your cat's measurements

A loose suit doesn't protect and a tight one hurts. We make it from your cat's measurements, with easy-open fastenings for incision checks — no wrestling with velcro, or with the cat.

  • For spay/neuter, abdominal wounds and post-surgery recovery
  • Easy-access fastenings for daily incision checks
  • Machine washable — worth having two during recovery

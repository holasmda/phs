Privacy notice

The simple version, no legalese: we only use your data to serve you. We never sell it or share it with third parties.

Who handles your data

Hotel Perros (San Miguel de Allende, Guanajuato) is responsible for the personal data you share with us.

What we collect

Only what we need to serve you: your name, your phone/WhatsApp number and, for home delivery, your address. If you send us photos of your pet, we use them only for the service you requested.

What we use it for

To reply to your messages, schedule and provide services, make deliveries and — only if you ask — send you harvest or availability alerts. Nothing else.

What we never do

We never sell, rent or share your data with third parties. We never send advertising you didn't ask for. We never use your photos without your permission.

Your rights

You can access, correct, delete or object to the use of your data at any time. Message us on WhatsApp or at our contact email and we'll take care of it.

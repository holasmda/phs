Services · In-home care

In-home pet sitting in San Miguel de Allende

Your pet stays home, on their routine — we come to them. Feeding and walk visits, or full overnight stays.

Drop-in visits (30–60 minutes)

Every visit includes: feeding, fresh water, a walk or playtime, litter box cleaning for cats, and photos on WhatsApp so you can see all is well. Book 1, 2 or 3 visits per day.

Overnight stays in your home

For dogs who get anxious alone at night, or when you travel for several days: we sleep at your house and your pet keeps their full routine. Your home stays occupied and looked after, too.

Perfect for cats

Cats hate leaving their territory. Instead of stressing them at a boarding facility, we come to your home: food, clean litter, playtime and cuddles (if the cat allows). That simple.

In-home care or boarding?

If your dog is social and loves to play, boarding or daycare suits them better. If they're anxious, a senior, or you have cats, in-home care is the right call. Not sure? Message us and we'll recommend based on your case.

Your pet at home, you at ease

Tell us on WhatsApp what pet you have and your dates, and we'll put together a visit plan.

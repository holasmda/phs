Dog daycare in San Miguel de Allende

A full day of supervised play, walks and rest — with pickup and drop-off included in weekly packages.

What a daycare day includes

  • Supervised play in small groups, matched by size and energy
  • Two walks a day
  • Mid-day rest time (a real nap, with beds)
  • Photos and a report via WhatsApp
  • Optional home pickup and drop-off

Hours

Monday to Saturday, 8:00 am to 6:00 pm. Half days available (morning or afternoon). Need a special schedule? Just ask on WhatsApp.

Small groups, happy dogs

We don't pack 30 dogs into a yard. We work with small, compatible groups so your dog plays without stress and comes home tired and happy — not overwhelmed.

Is daycare right for my dog?

Daycare is ideal for social dogs with energy to burn. If your dog is anxious, a senior, or prefers calm, take a look at our in-home care or overnight boarding instead. Need several days a week? Ask about our discounted packages.

The first meet-and-greet is free. Message us on WhatsApp and schedule a day to see the place.

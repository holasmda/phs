FAQ

What people ask us most

How do I measure my dog?

We need three: chest girth (the widest part, behind the front legs), back length (from the base of the neck to the base of the tail) and neck girth. We send you an illustrated guide on WhatsApp and check the photos with you.

What if it doesn't fit?

We do a fitting before delivery. If something still isn't right, we adjust it free of charge within the first 15 days.

Do you sell wholesale to vets?

Yes, we sell recovery suits wholesale to veterinary clinics in San Miguel de Allende. Message us on WhatsApp for the price list.

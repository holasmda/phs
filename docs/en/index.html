FAQ

What people ask us most

Another question? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll reply within 24 hours.

Do you accept all breeds and sizes?

Yes, as long as your dog is social — the meet-and-greet is exactly for confirming that. If your dog doesn't enjoy being around other dogs, we recommend in-home care instead.

Which vaccines are required?

Up-to-date rabies and DHPP. Puppies must have their full vaccine schedule. It's for the safety of every dog that visits us.

Do you also care for cats?

Yes, but in your home — cats get stressed away from their territory. We come to your house for feeding, a clean litter box and playtime. See our in-home care page.

How does home pickup work?

We collect your dog at your home in the morning and bring them back at the end of the day or their stay. Available in Centro and all colonias of San Miguel de Allende.

Can I visit before booking?

Of course — in fact we ask for it. The meet-and-greet is free and no-strings: your dog gets to know the place, and you get to know us.
